## About Me

I recently started my PhD at the Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e), where I am part of the Applied and Provable Security group, with as supervisor dr. Andreas Hülsing.

## Education

## Research Interests

In general, I do research in cryptography, specifically in post-quantum or quantum-resistant cryptography. During my Master's thesis, I worked on implementing and optimising SLH-DSA, which is a hash-based digital signature scheme standardised by NIST in [FIPS 205](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/205/final), before which the scheme was known as [SPHINCS+](https://sphincs.org/). My work now is essentially a continuation of my Master thesis, looking to provide security proofs of that implementation. This happens in collaboration with [Project Formosa Crypto](https://github.com/formosa-crypto), and their low-level programming language [Jasmin](https://github.com/jasmin-lang/jasmin) and proof assistant [EasyCrypt](https://github.com/EasyCrypt/easycrypt). Active efforts are underway to get my implementation into their [Libjade cryptographic library](https://github.com/formosa-crypto/formosa-libjade).
